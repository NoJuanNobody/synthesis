# Synthesis (the Banana Beater)

A handheld digital instrument with its own compute stack, car mode, a remix marketplace, and an agent-native streaming API over legal free music sources.

> **Status: concept only. Nothing is built yet.** This repo holds research, a draft API and schema, a Raspberry Pi 5 parts list, and a concept sketch.

## Credit
The MVP idea came from **Cow** (Instagram, handle as of Oct 2026), who is credited as the originator of the MVP concept.

## Licensing rules
- Tracks from Creative Commons and other free sources pass their **original license and attribution** through unchanged.
- **NonCommercial (NC) and NoDerivatives (ND) tracks are hard blocks** for commercial use and remixing.
- **Remix licenses are sold only for tracks creators upload themselves.**
- Marketplace "credits" are fixed-price platform credits, pending legal review. The token section of `RESEARCH.md` is **research, not legal advice**.

## Contents
- `RESEARCH.md`: compute modules, prior art, and token risk (research, not legal advice)
- `api/`: draft REST API (`API.md`), Postgres + pgvector schema (`schema.sql`, untested), hybrid search plan (`SEARCH.md`)
- `pi5-tier-parts-and-power.md`: Raspberry Pi 5 prototype parts and power budget
- `banana_beater_concept_v0.png`: concept sketch
